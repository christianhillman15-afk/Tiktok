# Offers & Pricing

> **The one idea to internalize:** The website is the **wedge**, the retainer is the **business**. You price the build near breakeven to win a skeptical stranger from TikTok, prove competence fast, and then ascend the client into recurring marketing — where the real profit lives. Never sell "a website." Sell *more calls, more booked jobs, and looking legit when someone Googles you at 9pm.*

This doc is the money map: the free lead magnet, the three productized build tiers, the one-time-vs-monthly decision, the retainer upsell that is the actual profit center, the guarantee stack that closes cold traffic, and exactly how to present price on a call.

## Rung 0 — The Free Teardown/Audit (your entire content engine points here)

Every video's CTA funnels to **one** thing: a free, personalized website teardown. This is the lowest-friction "yes" a skeptical owner will give a stranger, and it does three jobs at once:

1. **Starts the DM conversation** (see `06-lead-funnel-and-dm-templates.md`).
2. **Pre-frames the sale** — you diagnose 3 real flaws so the owner *feels the gap* before you ever quote a price.
3. **Withholds the fix** — you name the 3 conversion-killers but save the *how* for the live call: *"Want me to walk you through exactly how to fix these?"* That's the reason to book.

**How to produce it fast:** a 2–3 minute Loom/screen recording of their actual site (or use an audit tool like MyWebAudit/Insites to auto-generate a report and cut prep by 30–60 min). For the highest-intent leads, offer a **free homepage mockup preview** instead — highest commitment, highest close rate.

**Rule:** the audit is *always free.* Its job is to book the call, not to make money. Do not monetize it.

## Rungs 1–3 — The productized build ladder

Sell **outcomes, not features.** Name the tiers by result — **Get Found / Get Leads / Get Scale** — and publish a simple pricing page so impulse-driven TikTok leads can self-select without a long sales cycle. Flag **Growth as "MOST POPULAR"** and let **Pro anchor the price** so Growth feels like the obvious best value.

### Starter — "Get Found" (the wedge, priced near breakeven)
For an owner with **no site, a Facebook-only presence, or a broken-on-mobile site** who just needs to *look legit and get found.*
- 1–5 pages (Home / Services / About / Reviews / Contact)
- Mobile-first design (most TikTok traffic is mobile)
- Conversion copywriting on core pages
- **Click-to-call + lead form above the fold**
- Google Business Profile claim + optimization
- On-page SEO basics (titles, meta, schema, fast load)
- Hosting + SSL + backups, **unlimited small edits**
- **7-day launch**

**Deliberately low-margin.** Its only job is to win the client and prove you deliver. Lead cold buyers to the **$0 down + $149–$199/mo** option.

### Growth — "Get Leads" ⭐ (the anchor — most deals land here)
For an owner who wants the phone to actually ring.
- Everything in Starter, **plus:**
- 6–10 pages including **location & service pages** built for local SEO
- Custom design
- **Online booking/scheduling**
- **CRM with 60-second instant text-back** + email/SMS follow-up (this is what makes the site visibly *work*)
- Review-generation funnel
- Blog / CMS

**$3,500–$6,000 one-time** OR **$0 down + $249–$399/mo.** Position as the best value; most leads should land here.

### Pro — "Get Scale" (price anchor + light retainer)
For established businesses ready to dominate the metro.
- Everything in Growth, **plus:**
- 10+ pages, ecommerce or booking-with-payments
- Advanced integrations, A/B testing / CRO
- Full-site copywriting, priority support
- A **light marketing retainer included**

**$7,500–$15,000+ one-time** OR bundled into a **$1,500+/mo Growth Partner** retainer. Primarily an **anchor** — its job is to make Growth feel reasonable.

## One-time vs. Website-as-a-Service (WaaS): why monthly wins on TikTok

TikTok traffic is **impulse-driven, mobile, price-sensitive, and skeptical.** A $5,000 check kills the deal on the spot. **$0 down + a monthly fee** collapses that friction — and quietly 2–3x's your revenue per client.

**Offer BOTH prices on every tier and let the lead choose.** Most cold buyers pick monthly.

**The LTV math (use this to justify WaaS to yourself and to the client):**

| Model | Example | 24-month total | 36-month total |
|---|---|---|---|
| One-time build | $3,000 up front + ~$100/mo maintenance | ~$5,400 | ~$6,600 |
| **WaaS** | **$0 down + $199/mo** | **$4,776** | **$7,164** |

The honest pitch: *"It's not really cheaper over time — but there's no $5k check today, and we host it, maintain it, and update it forever."* Over 24 months a brochure site costs roughly the same either way, so WaaS isn't a discount — it's **removing the upfront-cash gamble**, which is the single biggest objection cold social leads have.

**Protect the monthly margin:** define **"unlimited small edits"** explicitly = text/image swaps and content updates, **not** new pages or full redesigns (those are scoped add-ons). Otherwise scope creep eats the fee.

## Rung 4 — The Growth Partner Retainer (the actual profit center)

The build proves competence; **recurring marketing is where you make real money.** Bake the ascension in from day one — put a line item on the *original* proposal so it's expected:

> *Growth Partner add-on: local SEO + Google Ads management — $1,500/mo (start month 2).*

Then in **month 2–3**, once the site is live and producing wins (the instant text-back is booking jobs, GBP calls are up), pitch the retainer against a concrete result.

**Retainer menu (à la carte):**

| Service | Price guidance | What's included |
|---|---|---|
| Local SEO | **$500–$1,500/mo** | GBP optimization, citations, review generation, local content |
| Google Ads management | **$750–$1,500/mo** or **15% of ad spend** (whichever is greater) | Campaign build, management, reporting (ad spend billed separately) |
| Social / content management | **$500–$1,500/mo** | Posting, content, community management |
| **All-in "Growth Partner" bundle** | **$1,500–$3,500/mo** | Priced *below* buying the pieces separately — the obvious choice |

**Guard the retainer with a scoped guarantee:** *"X new leads in 90 days or next month is free."* Tie it to a **controllable metric (leads/calls)** — never an unenforceable "#1 on Google" promise, which triggers refunds and bad reviews.

**Why this is the whole game:** clients with a nurtured relationship convert to upsell ~33% more, and retainers are predictable, compounding revenue. Track your **wedge-to-retainer ascension rate** (% of website clients on a retainer by month 3) as a core KPI.

## The guarantee stack (this is what closes cold TikTok traffic)

Skeptical strangers need risk *removed*, not features listed. Stack these and put one **in the video hook itself** ("$0 down, and if it doesn't get you more calls in 90 days we work free until it does"):

1. **Free custom homepage mockup before you pay a dime** — see it first, then decide.
2. **Don't-pay-until-you-love-it** — no payment until you approve the design.
3. **$0 down / pay-monthly** — this *is* risk-reversal; it removes the upfront gamble.
4. **30-day money-back** on the build.
5. **90-day leads guarantee** on retainers — *"X new leads or next month free."*
6. **Cancel anytime after a short 3–6 month minimum, and you own your domain + site.** Market this directly against competitors' predatory 36-month lock-ins where hostage hosting is a cancellation trap.

**Irresistible-offer framing (Hormozi value equation — Dream Outcome × Likelihood ÷ Time × Effort):**
- **Maximize dream outcome:** "a lead machine that books jobs while you sleep," not "a website."
- **Raise likelihood:** guarantee + before/after case studies.
- **Slash time:** "live in 7 days."
- **Slash effort/sacrifice:** "$0 down, done-for-you, we handle everything."

**Copy-paste offer script:**
> *"We'll build your new mobile website in 7 days — $0 down, just $199/month. That includes hosting, unlimited edits, and your Google Business Profile setup. If it doesn't get you more calls in 90 days, we work free until it does. Cancel anytime after 6 months, and you keep your domain."*

## How to present price on the discovery call

Follow the call structure in `06-lead-funnel-and-dm-templates.md`. On price specifically:

1. **Diagnose and quantify pain first.** *"You said you're getting maybe 2 jobs a month from online. What's an average job worth to you?"* Get them to say the number — if a job is $800 and the site brings 5 more/month, the ROI math makes the price trivial.
2. **Anchor high, recommend the middle.** Walk Pro → Growth → Starter. Recommend Growth (*"honestly, for what you described, Get Leads is the fit"*).
3. **Present the monthly, not the lump sum.** *"It's $0 down and $299 a month — less than one job pays for."*
4. **Lead with the guarantee, then the number.** De-risk *before* you say the price.
5. **Bake in ascension.** *"The site goes live in 7 days. Once it's booking jobs, in a month or two we can talk about the Growth Partner package to actually rank you on Google — but the site comes first."*
6. **One clear next step.** *"Want me to send the details today so we can launch by [date]?"*

## Pitfalls (these quietly kill margin and deals)

- **Selling "a website" instead of an outcome.** Leads want more calls, not web design.
- **Treating the build as your profit center.** If all your money is one-off builds, you re-sell every month instead of compounding retainers.
- **Predatory long lock-ins (36-month hard minimums, hostage hosting).** They generate refunds, chargebacks, and bad reviews from a skeptical TikTok audience — and competitors beat you with "cancel anytime, you own it."
- **WaaS churn with no retention hooks.** If a client cancels in month 4, you lose money on the build. Prevent it with unlimited edits, monthly reports, GBP management, and the retainer upsell that makes leaving painful.
- **Hiding prices behind a "book a call" form.** Impulse leads go cold — publish tiers so they self-qualify.
- **Vague guarantees** ("#1 on Google"). Unenforceable → refunds. Tie to controllable metrics.
- **Slow lead response on sites you build.** If a client's form leads sit unanswered they blame the site and churn — the 60-second instant text-back is non-negotiable on Growth+.
- **Undefined "unlimited edits."** Scope it to swaps, not redesigns, or the monthly fee bleeds out.
