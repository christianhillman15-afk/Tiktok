# Lead Funnel & DM/Sales Templates

This is the doc you keep open while you're actually working leads. It covers the whole path — **TikTok view → profile → free audit → DM → discovery call → close → follow-up** — and gives you the exact words to copy, paste, and send. Everything here maps to the six funnel stages in the strategy spine (Discover → Engage → Capture → Qualify & Book → Close → Follow-up & Ascend).

Rule of thumb before you use anything below: **we sell the free audit, never the build, in-video and in the comments. The build gets sold on the call. The retainer gets sold in month 2-3.** The site is the wedge; the retainer is the business.

---

## The funnel at a glance

| Stage | Where it happens | Your job | The one metric |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Discover | For You + Search | Get in front of right-fit local owners with teardowns / before-afters | Video completion (~70%) + keyword-phrase impressions |
| 2. Engage | Comments | Turn a watcher into an on-platform action | Comments/saves/shares per video |
| 3. Capture | DMs | Start the 1:1 and deliver the free audit | Qualified DMs / keyword DMs per week |
| 4. Qualify & Book | DMs → Calendly | Ask one fit question, send straight to the calendar | Discovery calls booked per week |
| 5. Close | Discovery call | Diagnose real pain, sell the wedge, seed the retainer | Wedge clients closed per month |
| 6. Follow-up & Ascend | Email/DM | Win the deals that stall, then ascend to retainer | Wedge-to-retainer rate by month 3 |

The funnel only works if the **top-of-funnel signal we track is qualified DMs and keyword comments — not views, not followers.** A video with 4,000 views and 12 "AUDIT" DMs beats a 400k-view video with none.

## 1. Profile setup (do this before you post a single video)

Your profile is the pivot point. Someone watches a teardown, thinks *"that's my site,"* and taps your name. In the next 3 seconds your bio has to say **what you do, who it's for, that you're legit, and exactly what to do next.**

### Business vs Creator account — the real decision

This is a genuine tradeoff, not a formality. Decide based on **whether your bottleneck is reach or conversion.**

| | Business account | Creator account |
|---|---|---|
| Clickable bio link | Yes, **no follower minimum** | Historically needed 1,000 followers (TikTok soft-rolling toward 0 since late 2025) |
| Contact / booking button | Yes | No |
| Analytics | Full | Limited |
| ManyChat / DM automation | **Eligible** | Not eligible |
| Music | **Commercial Music Library only** (royalty-free) | Full viral-sound library |
| Typical engagement | ~2.5–4% | ~11% |

**Our recommendation for Launch Media:**
- **If you're brand new and reach is your bottleneck** (you need the funnel to fill), start on **Creator** so you can ride viral sounds, and use a link hub or "DM me AUDIT" for capture until you cross ~1,000 followers.
- **The clean long-term setup:** run **two accounts** — a Creator personal account for growth/experiments, and a **Business account as your main sales machine** (clickable Calendly link + booking button + ManyChat automation). Most established local-agency operators land here because our KPI is *booked calls*, not vanity reach, and our teardown/before-after content doesn't live or die on trending audio.
- Do **not** sit on a Business account while reach is your bottleneck — you'll lose viral sounds and starve the top of the funnel.

### The bio formula

**[what you do] + [who it's for] + [proof/result] + [specific CTA]**

Copy-paste starters (swap in your real numbers and city — never inflate):

```
I build websites that book more jobs for [City] home-service pros
| 100+ launched | $0 down, live in 7 days
Free 3-min teardown of YOUR site 👇
```

```
Websites that make [City] contractors look legit + show up on Google
| Plumbers • HVAC • Roofers • Electricians
Free website teardown 👇 DM me the word AUDIT
```

```
Your competitor isn't better — their website just shows up first.
I fix that for [City] trades. 90-day leads guarantee.
Free audit 👇
```

Keep it in the owner's language — "book more jobs," "show up on Google," "look legit." Never "responsive CMS," "UX," or "SEO stack."

### The link — point it at a calendar, not a homepage

- The bio link goes **straight to your Calendly booking page**, not your agency homepage. Every extra click between interest and calendar tanks conversion.
- **UTM-tag it** so TikTok-sourced bookings are attributable: `...?utm_source=tiktok&utm_medium=bio&utm_campaign=free-audit`
- On the Calendly form, add a required field: **"How did you hear about us?"** — TikTok bookings are otherwise nearly impossible to attribute.
- If you want one hub (audit request + calendar + portfolio + reviews), use a single link page (Beacons/Stan/Bitly) — but the **primary button is still "Book my free teardown"** pointed at Calendly.

### Pin 3 videos — these sell before the DM

1. **A result / case study** — hard number up front. *"This [City] roofer went from 2 to 11 leads a month after we rebuilt his site."* (Pillar 4 — Proof.)
2. **A free-audit teardown of a real (anonymized) site** — shows exactly what the free audit is. *"3 things killing this [City] plumber's website."* (Pillar 1 — Teardowns.)
3. **A "how it works / what a project looks like" explainer** — $0 down, live in 7 days, you own it, cancel anytime. De-risks sending you money. (Pillar 5 — Offer/Process.)

A fence-sitter who lands on your profile from any single video should be able to see proof, see the free offer, and understand how to work with you — without leaving the profile.

## 2. The free-audit lead magnet (the thing the whole engine points at)

The entire content machine exists to get owners to raise their hand for **one free thing:** a personalized look at their actual site. It's the entry rung of the offer ladder. **It is free on purpose — the point is to start the conversation and pre-frame the discovery call, not to monetize the audit.**

### Three formats (pick by intent level)

| Format | What it is | Effort | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Free 3-point audit** | Checklist-style: 3 concrete conversion-killers on their site | Low (5–10 min) | High volume, fast turnaround |
| **Free website teardown** | 2–3 min screen-recorded Loom walking their real site | Medium | The default — most persuasive per minute |
| **Free homepage mockup / redesign preview** | A quick visual of what their homepage *could* look like | High | Highest intent, hottest leads — offer selectively |

### The mechanic that makes it work: **withhold the fixes**

You **name the 3 problems but do not hand over the full fixes.** The full walkthrough is the reason to book the call.

> "I found 3 things on your site that are costing you calls right now. Two are quick wins on your homepage, one's a mobile issue. Want me to walk you through exactly how to fix them on a quick 15-min call?"

### Tooling to make it fast

- Use **MyWebAudit** or **Insites** to auto-generate a report (cuts call prep 30–60 min and surfaces real dollar-value gaps), **or** just record a **Loom** teardown manually.
- Keep a **teardown checklist** so every audit hits the same owner-brain points: works on mobile? click-to-call above the fold? lead form present? loads fast? reviews shown? shows up when you Google the business name? Google Business Profile claimed?

### Delivery — this trips people up

**You cannot put a clickable link inside a TikTok DM.** So the flow is:

1. Capture their **email in the DM**.
2. **Deliver the teardown + booking link by email.**
3. Keep talking in the DM to book the call.

## 3. CTA phrasings (video + caption)

Every video stacks **two CTAs, in this order:**

1. **Engagement CTA first** (boosts reach, self-identifies intent) — a comment ask.
2. **Fulfillment CTA second** — how they actually get the free audit (DM the keyword, or the link).

### The US/UK/EU gotcha — read this twice

**TikTok's native comment-to-DM automation is NOT live in the US/UK/EU** (only Vietnam/Thailand/Indonesia as of early 2026). So **never say "comment AUDIT and I'll auto-DM you"** — those leads get nothing and you look broken.

Two legit paths:
- **ManyChat path (scales):** tell viewers to **"DM me the word AUDIT"** so the ManyChat keyword flow fires. Requires a **Business account, DMs set to "Everyone."**
- **Manual path (small accounts):** tell viewers to comment, then **you manually DM every commenter.** Fine at low volume, doesn't scale.

### The two-CTA stack (spoken, in-video)

> "Comment your trade so more [City] owners see this — then **DM me the word AUDIT** and I'll send you a free teardown of your site with the 3 things costing you calls. No pitch."

Pick **ONE keyword** and burn it in everywhere — **AUDIT** is our default. Never use "hi" or "help" (too generic, false triggers). Test one CTA per week; track DM volume and link clicks.

### Copy-paste CTA library

| Use | Phrasing |
|---|---|
| **Default video close** | "DM me the word **AUDIT** and I'll send you a free teardown of YOUR site — 3 things costing you calls, no pitch." |
| **Engagement-first (reach)** | "Comment your city and I'll tell you the #1 thing killing your leads." |
| **Niche self-ID** | "Comment your trade and I'll tell you the first thing I'd fix on your site." |
| **Scarcity / local** | "Taking **3 [City] home-service businesses** this month — comment your city if you want a spot." |
| **Booking urgency** | "Book the free teardown before Friday — link in bio (straight to my calendar)." |
| **Mockup (high intent)** | "Want to SEE what your homepage could look like? DM **AUDIT** and I'll mock one up free." |

**Avoid:** "link in bio" *(alone)*, "DM us to learn more," "contact us for a quote." Generic CTAs waste the click — always name the specific payoff.

### Caption formula

Front-load the target search phrase, then the CTA:

```
[City] plumber websites are losing calls at 9pm — here's the fix.
DM me AUDIT for a free teardown of yours. #[city]plumber #localbusiness #websitedesign #smallbusinesstips
```

3–5 hashtags: broad + niche + local.

## 4. The DM playbook

### Ground rules (non-negotiable)

1. **Never open with the ask.** No "want to hop on a call?" as the first line. Lead with the free teardown. Personalized DMs get ~32% response vs ~18% for generic cold outreach.
2. **Follow the 5-beat structure:** warm acknowledgment → deliver value → one qualifying question → recommend the next step → send the booking link.
3. **No clickable links in DMs.** Capture email, deliver by email.
4. **Cap active-deal follow-up at ~3 touches**, 2–3 days apart. More than that and you're pushy.
5. **ManyChat limits:** Business account only, DMs on "Everyone," **10 messages per 48-hour window** (window resets on each user reply). Don't burn messages.

### ManyChat keyword auto-flow (viewer DMs "AUDIT")

This is the automated front door. Keep it to 3 of your 10 messages so you have room to keep talking.

```
Msg 1: Hey! Happy to run your free website teardown 🙌
        What's the best email to send it to?

Msg 2: Perfect — sending your teardown to {email} now.
        Check your inbox in a few minutes 📩

Msg 3: Quick one while you're here: what's the #1 thing you
        wish your website did better right now?
```

Their reply to Msg 3 hands you the pain point and reopens a fresh 48-hour window. From there you go manual.

---

### Template A — Inbound opener (they DMed/commented the keyword)

Use after the auto-flow, or as your manual first reply. Beats 1–2–4–5 of the structure.

```
Awesome — just sent your teardown over to your email 🙌

I spotted a couple quick wins on your homepage and one thing
on mobile that's almost certainly losing you clicks (people
Googling you at 9pm can't easily call you).

Want me to walk you through exactly how to fix them on a quick
15-min call this week? Zero pitch — you keep the teardown either way.

Here's my calendar: [Calendly link]
```

Worked example (filled in), so you can see the tone:

```
Awesome Mike — just sent your teardown over to mike@aceplumbingdenver.com 🙌
I spotted two quick wins on your homepage and one mobile issue that's
losing you calls after hours. Want me to walk you through the fixes on a
quick 15-min call this week? You keep the teardown either way.
Here's my calendar: calendly.com/launchmedia/teardown
```

---

### Template B — Qualify (one question, then read the answer)

Ask **one** pain/fit question. You're not interrogating — you're finding the trigger and gauging budget-readiness without saying "budget."

```
Before I send anything — quick q so I make it useful:
right now, are you getting a steady flow of calls through the
website, or is it mostly word of mouth and referrals?
```

Alternate qualifiers (pick one):

```
What made you look at this now — competitor showing up above you,
a rebrand, or just tired of the current site?
```

```
When someone gets referred to you and Googles your name — are you
happy with what they see?
```

Read the answer for the **buying trigger** (competitor outranking, rebrand, embarrassment moment, lost deal). If they name one, that's a hot lead — move to book.

---

### Template C — Book-the-call

Once there's interest, make booking the obvious next step and remove friction. Offer a binary time choice, not an open-ended "when works?"

```
Easiest way to get you the full breakdown + the 3 fixes done-with-you
is a quick 15 min — no pressure, no pitch.

Do mornings or afternoons work better for you? Here's the link to
grab a slot: [Calendly link]
```

**Booking nudge** (interested but hasn't booked):

```
No pressure at all — the audit's yours to keep either way 🙌
But if you want the full breakdown + the fixes mapped out, 15 minutes
is the fastest way. Mornings or afternoons better for you?
```

---

### Template D — No-show follow-up (recovery)

Send within a couple hours of the missed slot. Assume good intent — owners are on the tools all day.

```
Hey [Name], looks like we missed each other today — no worries at all,
I know you're busy running the business 🙌

I've still got your teardown ready to walk through. Here's my link to
grab a new time whenever works: [Calendly link]
```

If still nothing after 2 days:

```
Still happy to walk you through those 3 fixes whenever you get a window,
[Name]. Want me to hold a spot for next week, or is now not the right time?
```

---

### Template E — "Not now" nurture

For a lead who got the audit or took the call but says timing isn't right. **This lead does NOT go into the 3-touch active cadence** — you move them to a lighter, longer-spaced value touch so you're front-of-mind when their trigger hits.

Immediate reply:

```
Totally fair, [Name] — no rush. The teardown's yours to keep, and the
fixes will still be there when you're ready.

Mind if I check back in a month or so? And if a competitor's site starts
outranking you before then, just DM me — that's usually the sign it's time.
```

~30-day value touch (send something useful, not "just checking in"):

```
Hey [Name] — thought of you. Just published a quick breakdown on the
#1 thing costing [trade] businesses calls after hours [context/topic].
No ask — figured it'd be useful given what we saw on your site. Still here
whenever you want those fixes done.
```

Trigger-based re-open (when you notice a rebrand, new location, or a competitor move):

```
Saw [trigger — new location / rebrand / competitor ranking above you].
That's exactly the moment a site earns its money. Want me to re-run your
teardown with that in mind? Free, same as before.
```

---

### Template F — Value-first cold outreach (prospecting)

For proactively DMing right-fit local owners you find (not inbound). Full 5-beat structure. **Lead with value, never the ask.** Personalize beat 1 with a real detail — this is what gets ~32% response instead of getting ignored.

```
1. Hey [Name] — came across [business] here in [City], love what you're
   doing with [specific real detail — e.g. the reviews, the truck wrap,
   the service area].

2. I actually recorded a quick 2-min teardown of your site with 3 things
   that are likely costing you calls (one's a mobile issue most folks miss).
   Happy to send it over — no strings, you keep it either way.

3. Quick q: are you getting many enquiries through the site right now, or
   is it mostly word of mouth?

4. If it's useful, I can walk you through the fixes on a quick 15-min call.

5. Here's my calendar if you want to grab a time: [Calendly link]
```

Note: send beats 1–3 first, get a reply, **then** send 4–5. Don't dump all five at once cold.

## 5. Discovery-call agenda & framework

30 minutes, structured. **The bulk is diagnosis, not pitching.** You lead with the free mockup and the guarantee stack, sell the *outcome* ("a lead machine that books jobs while you sleep"), and you put the Growth Partner retainer on the proposal so ascension is expected from day one.

| Segment | Time | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Agenda | 2–3 min | Set the frame, lower the guard |
| 2. Rapport | 2–3 min | Human connection |
| 3. Diagnose (situation + pain) | **10–15 min** | The bulk — find the real pain & trigger |
| 4. Map the solution | 5 min | Connect their pain to the right package |
| 5. Lock next step | 3–5 min | Get a decision or a clear next action |

### 1. Agenda (set the frame)

```
"Thanks for jumping on. Here's how I like to run these so I don't waste
your time: first I want to hear about your business and what's actually
going on, then I'll walk you through what I found on your site, and if it
makes sense I'll show you exactly what I'd do to fix it. If it's a fit,
great — if not, you keep the teardown and we part as friends. Sound good?"
```

### 2. Rapport (2–3 min)

Keep it real and short. Ask how long they've been running it, how business is. **Validate the hustle** ("word of mouth got you this far — that's not luck, that's a good business") before you show any gap. Never insult their success.

### 3. Diagnose — the bulk (10–15 min)

Open with the same line every time:

```
"Before I show you what I found — tell me about your business and who
you're trying to reach."
```

Then dig for **situation → pain → trigger → cost.** Questions:

- "Where do most of your jobs come from right now?"
- "When a referral Googles your name at 9pm, what do you think they see?"
- "What made you want the teardown — was there a specific moment?" *(hunting for the trigger: competitor, rebrand, embarrassment, lost deal)*
- "What's one new customer actually worth to you over a year?" *(this is your price anchor — get the number)*
- "If your phone rang 5 more times a week from the site, what would that mean for the business?"

**Shut up and listen.** You're getting the exact language you'll sell back to them, plus the dollar value of one customer that makes the price a non-issue.

### 4. Map the solution (5 min)

Now — and only now — connect what you heard to a package. Sell the outcome, not features. Lead with the **free mockup + guarantee stack**:

```
"Based on what you told me, here's what I'd build you: a site that shows
up when someone searches '[trade] near me' in [City], lets them tap-to-call
in one thumb-press, and captures the 76% of those people who call within a
day. You told me one customer is worth [$X] — this needs to land you one
extra job to pay for itself, and it's built to land a lot more than one.

And here's how we take the risk off you: $0 down, we build it in 7 days,
you don't pay until you love it, you own it, cancel anytime, and there's a
90-day leads guarantee behind it."
```

**Package logic** (per the offer ladder):
- Present **three tiers**, anchor with **Pro ("Get Scale")** at the top so Growth feels reasonable.
- Steer most owners to **Growth ("Get Leads") — flagged MOST POPULAR** — it has the location/service pages, online booking, CRM with 60-second text-back, and review funnel that actually move the needle.
- **Lead cold TikTok buyers to the monthly WaaS option** ($0 down + $249–399/mo for Growth; $149–199/mo for Starter). Monthly kills the lump-sum sticker shock and 2–3x's revenue per client over the term.
- **Put the Growth Partner retainer line on the proposal** (even if not sold today) so ascension is expected. Don't hard-pitch marketing on call one — the site is the wedge.

### 5. Lock the next step (3–5 min)

Close with the two lines that reliably work:

```
"What other questions can I answer for you while we're here?"
```

(Better than "any questions?" — it invites, doesn't close the door.)

Then:

```
"Based on everything you told me, [Growth] is the best fit. Want me to
send the details over today so you can look while it's fresh?"
```

Get a **specific next action with a date** before you hang up — proposal sent today, decision by Friday, kickoff [month]. A call that ends with "let me think about it" and no date is a call you'll lose in follow-up.

## 6. Objection-handling snippets

Use these in DMs, on the call, and (reworked) as objection-busting content. Always **validate first, then reframe in the owner's language** — never web jargon, never insult what's working.

**"I have a Facebook page."**
> "Facebook's great for staying in touch — keep it. But it's a house you built on rented land: Meta owns it, only about 16% of your followers even see your posts, it barely shows up on Google, and it can't take a booking or a deposit at 9pm. A website is land you actually own."

**"I get all my business by word of mouth."**
> "And that's the strongest kind — don't change that. But here's the thing: word of mouth ends on Google now. The second someone refers you, their friend types your name in. If nothing convincing shows up, that warm referral dies right there. A site doesn't replace word of mouth — it *closes* it."

**"It's too expensive / I don't have the cash."**
> "Totally get it. What's one new customer worth to you over a year — you said about [$X]? This is $0 down and [$249]/mo, so it just has to land you one extra job to pay for itself. Everything after that is profit. No lump sum, no sticker shock."

**"I tried Wix myself."**
> "You didn't fail at Wix — you found out it's a 40-hour time sink that still doesn't show up on Google. Your time's worth more running the business than fighting a website builder. We do it all for you and launch in 7 days."

**"I don't have time for this."**
> "That's exactly why to hand it to us. 10-minute intake, we handle the copy, the photos, the launch — you barely lift a finger. Time-poor is the reason to buy, not to wait."

**"I've been burned by an agency / I don't trust this."**
> "Fair — a lot of agencies deserve that. That's why it's $0 down, you don't pay until you love it, you own the site, and you can cancel anytime. If I don't deliver, you're not stuck. The risk is on me, not you."

**"I don't think I need a website / not relevant to my trade."**
> "97% of people search online before they hire a local pro, and 76% of 'near me' searches call within a day. If a ready-to-buy customer looks for a [trade] in [City] tonight and can't find you, they're calling whoever *does* show up. That's not a website problem — it's a lost-customer problem."

**"Let me think about it."**
> "Of course — it's your money and your business. What specifically do you want to think through? Usually it's one of three things: is it worth it, will it actually work, or is now the right time — happy to talk through whichever it is right now." *(surfaces the real objection instead of a stall)*

## 7. Follow-up cadence

**Most deals die in follow-up, not on the call.** Speed and a clear next step win them back. Cap active deals at ~3 touches, 2–3 days apart, then a break-up.

| Touch | Timing | Channel | Message |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Recap** | **Within 24h** | Email (+DM ping) | Their pain → your solution → 1–2 benefits → clear next step + proposal |
| **Touch 2** | +2–3 days | Email/DM | Nudge on the proposal, propose a start month |
| **Touch 3 (break-up)** | +2–3 days | DM/Email | The "close your file?" question that re-triggers replies |

### Recap (send within 24h — this one is decisive)

```
Great chatting, [Name]! Quick recap:

You want [outcome — more calls / to stop losing referrals on Google].
The main thing holding that back is [pain].
The [Growth] site fixes it by [benefit 1] and [benefit 2] — and it's
$0 down, live in 7 days, with the 90-day leads guarantee behind it.

Proposal's attached. Happy to start [month] — anything you'd want
tweaked before we lock it in?
```

### Touch 2 (proposal nudge)

```
Hey [Name] — did the proposal make sense? Happy to answer anything.
If you're good to go, I can have your site live within 7 days of kickoff.
Want to start [month]?
```

### Touch 3 (break-up — reliably restarts replies)

```
Hey [Name] — haven't heard back, so I'm guessing the timing isn't right,
which is totally fine. Want me to close out your file, or should I hold
your spot for [month]?
```

That question format works because it's easy to answer and it puts a small decision in front of them. After touch 3 with no reply, move them to the **"not now" nurture** (Template E) — don't keep hammering.

## 9. Weekly operator checklist

- [ ] 4 videos posted (fixed schedule), each with the **two-CTA stack** (comment → DM AUDIT)
- [ ] Worked my own comments in the **first 30–60 min** (golden hour) on every post
- [ ] Every keyword DM answered with **value first** (Template A), never an ask
- [ ] One qualifying question asked per live DM thread (Template B)
- [ ] Bio link still points **straight at Calendly**, UTM intact
- [ ] Every discovery call recapped **within 24h** (Template recap)
- [ ] Active-deal follow-ups capped at 3, break-up sent where due
- [ ] "Not now" leads moved to nurture, not abandoned
- [ ] Numbers logged: **qualified DMs, calls booked, wedge clients closed, wedge-to-retainer rate**

**The whole doc in one line:** get the raise-the-hand DM with a free teardown, deliver value before you ever ask, book straight to the calendar, diagnose more than you pitch, sell the wedge with the risk on you, and never let a warm lead die in slow follow-up.
