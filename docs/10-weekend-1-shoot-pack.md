# Weekend 1 Shoot Pack — film these 8 in one sitting

> The playbook is useless until you post. This is the "just pick up your phone" asset: **8 complete, ready-to-film videos** that cover all five content pillars, ordered so you can batch-film the whole thing in about **90 minutes**. Two weeks of content in one afternoon.

**Default trade: plumbers** (biggest pool, most relatable, easiest teardowns). To run a different trade, swap the word "plumber" for "roofer / HVAC / electrician / landscaper" everywhere — the structure is identical. Better yet, film each of these for 2–3 trades and you've got a month of content.

---

## Before you hit record (5-min setup)

- **Gear:** phone (rear camera), a $15 tripod, and a window or ring light in front of your face (never behind you).
- **Framing:** vertical 9:16, face in the top third, eyes at lens height.
- **Audio:** quiet room; phone within ~3 ft, or a cheap lav mic. Audio matters more than video.
- **Captions:** film clean, add **burned-in captions in CapCut** after (adds ~12% retention *and* feeds TikTok search).
- **Delivery:** one clean take per video is fine — energy > polish. Restart if you flub the first 3 seconds; that's the only part that must be perfect.
- **The one rule:** say the **trade + "website"** out loud in your first line so you rank in search.

### Batch-film order (do it this way)
1. **Films 1, 4, 6, 7, 8 first** — all talking-head, same setup, back to back. Just change your shirt between a couple if you want them to look like different days.
2. **Then Films 2, 3, 5** — screen-recordings/teardowns. Record your screen scrolling a real (blurred) or example site, talk over it.
3. **Then** drop all 8 into CapCut, add captions, export.
4. **Schedule** across ~2 weeks per the calendar in `03-content-calendar-30-days.md` (Tue/Wed/Thu/Sat).

> ⚠️ Anonymize any real business you critique — blur the name/logo, or roast a generic example / your own old work. Never punch at a named small business.

## After you post

- **Work the golden hour:** reply to every comment in the first 30–60 min. Every "how much for a roofer?" comment is your next video — answer it with a **Video Reply**.
- **Pin Film 1, 2, and 5** to your profile — they sell before anyone DMs you.
- **Route the keywords:** when someone comments `AUDIT` / `WEBSITE` / `SPOT`, follow the DM flow in `06-lead-funnel-and-dm-templates.md` (US: tell them to *DM* the word so ManyChat fires; deliver the teardown + booking link by email).
- **Don't judge results for 90 days.** Some of these won't move for a week, then rank in search and pull leads for months. Never delete a "flop."
