# Ready-to-Film Video Scripts

Fifteen copy-paste scripts you can shoot on a phone tomorrow. Every script serves the same machine described in the strategy spine: **Launch Media builds websites that book more jobs for home-service and trades businesses — anywhere in the US — then runs the marketing that keeps their phone ringing.** These videos get in front of right-fit trades owners across the country with buyer-intent content, earn an on-platform action, then move the self-identified lead into a DM and onto a discovery call for the **Free Website Teardown**. We sell the free audit in-video — never the build.

The mix below is weighted the way the spine demands: **Teardowns/Roasts (Pillar 1) and Before/Afters (Pillar 2) carry most of the volume**, education (Pillar 3) and proof (Pillar 4) do the trust and closing work, and only **2 of 15 (~13%) are offer-forward** (Pillar 5) — safely under the 20–30% ceiling.

---

## How to use this file

**The one thing you fill in — the trade:**
- **The trade** is the vertical you're targeting (plumber, HVAC, electrician, roofer, landscaper, cleaner, garage-door, pest control). **Say it out loud in the first line and burn it into the on-screen text.** This is the single most important word in every video — it's how the right owner self-identifies ("that's me") and how the algorithm learns which trade to resurface you to.
- **National reach, local clients — hold both in your head.** Launch Media serves home-service & trades businesses *anywhere in the US*, so we niche by **trade, not by city**. But every client is still a local business, so keep the "near me / when someone Googles you / get found in your town / Google Business Profile" language whenever you're describing *the client's* customers and market. Our reach is national; their customers are down the street.
- Run **one vertical per series** — if you're doing "plumber week," make the teardown, the before/after, and the price video all about plumbers so the content resurfaces to plumbers.

**Format legend** (matches the spine's format hierarchy):
- **HYBRID** = face-cam bubble (corner) over a screen recording. Use for teardowns, before/afters, and any "money" video — screen proves skill, face builds the trust needed to sell a $3k–$10k service.
- **TALKING HEAD** = just you to camera, phone on a tripod, good light. Use for opinions, myth-busting, offers, BTS.
- **VOICEOVER** = faceless screen recording with your voice. Use to keep volume high (batch 5 teardowns in a session).

**Production non-negotiables:** shoot vertical on a phone with good light, burn in **accurate** captions (auto-captions lie — fix them), 3-second hook, fast cuts, original audio (you talking). Aim for ~70% completion. Blur or anonymize any real business you didn't build — **never roast a named, identifiable business.**

### The CTA + automation mechanic (read once, applies to every script)

Native comment-to-DM automation is **NOT live in the US/UK/EU** — so we do not say "comment X and I'll auto-DM you." Instead every offer CTA stacks two moves:

1. **Engagement first (for reach):** "Comment `[WORD]`" (or "comment your trade / your #1 fix") — this feeds the algorithm and lets you reply publicly so others see the offer.
2. **Capture (to fire the funnel):** "…then **DM me the word `[WORD]`**" — this is what actually triggers ManyChat (Business account, DMs set to Everyone). Because DMs can't hold clickable links, ManyChat captures the email in-DM and **delivers the teardown + Calendly booking link by email.**

Each script uses a **different keyword** on purpose — it all routes to the same free-teardown flow, but the word tells you *which video earned the DM* (attribution the spine cares about). Configure all 15 keywords in ManyChat once. Booking link goes **straight to the UTM'd Calendly page, never the homepage.**

> Say it naturally on camera, e.g.: *"Comment ROAST so this reaches more plumbers, then DM me the word ROAST and I'll send your free teardown straight to your inbox — no pitch."*
