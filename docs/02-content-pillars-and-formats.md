# Content Pillars & Formats

> Companion to the Strategy Spine (Doc 01). Everything here expands the five pillars named in the Spine. Nothing here overrides it. If this doc and the Spine ever disagree, the Spine wins.

This is the content engine for Launch Media: the agency that **builds websites that book more jobs for local home-service and trades businesses in [City] — then runs the marketing that keeps their phone ringing.** The website is the wedge; the retainer is the business. Every video below exists to do one of two jobs: get in front of a right-fit owner who Googles their trade at 9pm, or move a self-identified owner one step closer to commenting **AUDIT** and starting a DM.

**Read this before you script anything:**

- We sell the **outcome** (more calls, booked jobs, looking legit), never the feature ("responsive CMS").
- We sell the **free audit** in-video, never the build. The build gets sold on the discovery call.
- We **never roast a named or identifiable business.** Blur it, anonymize it, or roast our own old work.
- We stay in **one vertical per series.** A plumber has to see a plumber's site to book. "I build websites for small businesses" is too broad to make anyone feel seen.
- We optimize for **qualified DMs, booked calls, saves and shares** — not views or followers.

## The pillar mix at a glance

Cadence is **4 posts/week on a fixed schedule (Tue / Wed / Thu / Sat), sustained for 90 days before judging results.** That's ~20 posts per 5-week cycle. Here's how the pillars split across that cycle.

| # | Pillar | Funnel job | Best format | Share of posts | Per 20-post cycle | Offer-forward? |
|---|--------|-----------|-------------|----------------|-------------------|----------------|
| 1 | Website Teardowns / Roasts | Discover — reach + authority | Faceless screen-record (voiceover); hybrid for flagship | **30%** | 6 | No |
| 2 | Before / After Redesign Reveals | Engage — desire + intent | Before/after split-screen or time-lapse, usually with face-cam bubble | **25%** | 5 | No |
| 3 | Objection-Busting & Plain-Language Education | Discover/Capture — trust + search ranking | Talking head (screen-record for the Google test) | **15%** | 3 | No |
| 4 | Proof / Client Results & Case Studies | Close/Follow-up — proof that converts | Talking head leading with the number + screen-record proof | **10%** | 2 | Soft |
| 5 | Offer, Process & Founder BTS | Capture/Close — de-risk + conversion | Talking head (your real face) | **20%** | 4 | Yes (soft-sell the audit) |

**Guardrails baked into this mix:**

- **70-80% educational/entertaining, 20-30% offer-forward.** Pillars 1-4 (80%) travel and build trust; only Pillar 5 (20%) is offer-forward, and even that soft-sells the *free audit*, never the build.
- **Teardowns + before/afters together = 55% of all posts.** These are the two pillars that reliably generate inbound DMs. Weight the calendar toward them.
- Don't force the mix week-by-week — hit the ratio over the rolling cycle. Proof content is gated by real results and client permission, so it's the lowest-volume pillar on purpose. Never fabricate a case study to hit a quota.

**A sample 2-week rhythm (8 posts):**

| Day | Week 1 | Week 2 |
|-----|--------|--------|
| Tue | Teardown | Teardown |
| Wed | Before/After | Education (objection) |
| Thu | Offer/BTS (free audit) | Before/After |
| Sat | Education (Google-at-9pm) | Proof (client win) |

## Pillar 1 — Website Teardowns / Roasts

**Funnel stage:** Discover (For You + Search). This is the **highest-volume pillar** and the top of the whole funnel.

**Purpose:** Demonstrate expertise on a concrete, real example so owners self-identify — "my site does that too" — in the comments. That self-identification is the entire point: it feeds the algorithm (comments + saves), surfaces warm leads by name, and ranks for "[trade] website" searches. A teardown is a live commercial for the free audit without ever pitching it.

**Best format:** Primarily **faceless screen-recording with voiceover** — it keeps production friction low so you can hold a 4/week cadence, and the screen itself proves competence. Cursor-point every flaw. For your one flagship teardown per cycle, use the **hybrid (face-cam bubble in the corner over the screen recording)** because the money videos need your face to build the trust that sells a $3k-$6k build. Always **blur or anonymize the brand.**

**Length:** 30-60s. Hook in the first 3 seconds. Aim ~70% completion.

**6-10 video ideas (niche the series to ONE trade at a time):**

1. "3 things killing this [City] plumber's website" — cursor-pointed screen recording, brand blurred.
2. "POV: you paid your nephew $200 to build your HVAC site" — narrate the predictable mistakes.
3. "Rating [City] roofer websites until I find one that doesn't need me" — series format, several sites in one video.
4. "This [City] electrician is losing calls because of this one button" — single-flaw deep dive (no tap-to-call on mobile).
5. "This landscaper's site takes 8 seconds to load — here's what that costs him" — load-speed teardown with the lost-jobs math.
6. "Your hero says 'Welcome to our website.' Here's what it should say instead." — headline teardown across 3 anonymized trades sites.
7. "I opened this cleaning company's site on my phone and immediately closed it — here's why" — mobile-first teardown (the broken-on-mobile pain).
8. "This garage-door site has no way to actually contact them" — the missing click-to-call + form-above-the-fold teardown.
9. "Rebuilding this pest-control hero section live in 90 seconds" — teardown that rolls straight into a mini-fix.
10. "Drop your website in the comments and I'll roast it" — UGC series (see Signature Formats). Infinite content + every link is a self-identified lead.

**Keyword CTA:** "Comment **ROAST** and I'll do this to your site for free" or the engagement-first "Comment your trade and I'll tell you your #1 site fix." Work the golden hour — reply to every comment in the first 30-60 minutes; answer the best buyer question with a Video Reply that becomes its own searchable post.

## Pillar 2 — Before / After Redesign Reveals

**Funnel stage:** Engage. **Weighted heavily alongside teardowns** — the two carry your reach and your intent.

**Purpose:** The most **save- and share-worthy** service format there is. It delivers instant visual payoff and lets the owner imagine their own glow-up — "that could be my shop." Teardowns get them to self-identify with the *problem*; before/afters get them to desire the *solution*. Saves are the highest-value signal we track ("I'll hire this person later").

**Best format:** **Before/after split-screen or time-lapse**, with the satisfying reveal held to the end (open loop). Add a **face-cam bubble** on the ones you want to carry trust. Burn a one-line business result over the "after" whenever you have one ("5x the calls").

**Length:** 20-45s for a pure reveal; up to 60s if you narrate the decisions.

**6-10 video ideas:**

1. "I redesigned a [City] med-spa site in 3 hours — before vs after" (time-lapse, satisfying reveal at the end).
2. "$500 website vs $5,000 website — same business, split screen" (see Signature Formats; doubles as budget qualifier).
3. "From this... to booked out — same shop, new site, 5x the calls" with a one-line result overlay.
4. "Watch me turn this dead Facebook-only trades business into a real site in 60 seconds" (the Facebook-page-only pain, visualized).
5. "This is what your site looks like to a customer at 9pm — and this is the fix" (before = dated/broken, after = clean + click-to-call).
6. "Turning a broken-on-mobile plumber site into a lead machine — before/after" (phone-frame both states).
7. "Ugly hero → booking button above the fold in 30 seconds" — micro before/after focused on one high-leverage change.
8. "Same roofer, same photos, new site — here's the difference a week makes" (7-day launch subtext).
9. "Before: no reviews anywhere. After: reviews front and center + a review funnel" — the credibility glow-up.
10. "I gave this HVAC company's homepage a $0-down glow-up" — reveal that quietly frames the WaaS offer.

**Keyword CTA:** "Want your before/after? Comment **AUDIT** and I'll show you 3 things to fix first." Pin the strongest before/after as a "start here" video.

## Pillar 3 — Objection-Busting & Plain-Language Education

**Funnel stage:** Discover → Capture. **Trust + search ranking.**

**Purpose:** Answer the exact buyer-intent phrases owners type into TikTok-as-Google, and dissolve the four real objections — *relevance, "I have Facebook," word-of-mouth, price* — in the owner's own language. Every video here is an **evergreen search asset** that keeps pulling for 6-12 months. This is where you rank in low-competition searches within 1-3 days as the big fish in the [City] trades pond.

**Best format:** **Talking head** — opinions and myth-busting need your face and conviction ("people book people"). Use a quick **screen-recording** only for the Google-at-9pm demo where you literally show what a searcher sees. Say the target phrase out loud in the first line, put it in the caption front-loaded, and burn it into on-screen text.

**Length:** 30-60s. This is your search bread-and-butter — tight and keyword-front-loaded.

**6-10 video ideas (each targets one phrase an owner would type):**

1. "The Google-at-9pm test: a customer just heard about you and typed your name in — here's what they see." (screen-record the sad result, then the fix.)
2. "'I have a Facebook page' — why that's a house built on rented land" (you don't own it, ~16% see it, nobody can book at 9pm).
3. "Word of mouth is amazing — but it ends on Google" (the site *closes* referrals, it doesn't replace them — validate their hustle first).
4. "How much a small-business website should actually cost in 2026" + the one-customer price math.
5. "Small business website vs just a Facebook page — which one actually books jobs?"
6. "Why your business isn't showing up on Google Maps (and the 2-minute fix)."
7. "You don't need a 10-page website — you need a one-page site that does THIS."
8. "Stop paying $30/month for a Wix site that doesn't get you found."
9. "3 mistakes killing your website's conversion rate" (15-30s quick-tip).
10. "If your site does THIS on mobile, you're handing jobs to your competitor."

**Keyword CTA:** Soft engagement CTA to farm comments and intent — "Comment your industry and I'll tell you your #1 site fix." Mine every comment for the next 10 scripts; each question is a video that ranks for the next person asking it.

## Pillar 4 — Proof / Client Results & Case Studies

**Funnel stage:** Close / Follow-up. **Bottom-funnel proof that converts fence-sitters.**

**Purpose:** This is the **pinned, DM-follow-up, deal-closing** content. It leads with the **business outcome** (calls, jobs, revenue) — never aesthetics. Buyers purchase revenue, not typography. "This roofer went from 2 to 11 leads a month" converts; "look how clean this is" doesn't. Lowest-volume pillar because it's honestly gated by real results and client permission — never fake one.

**Best format:** **Talking head leading with the hard number** (say it in the first 3 seconds), backed by **screen-recorded proof** — the client's "phone won't stop ringing" text, Google Business Profile call-clicks before vs after. Reaction/reveal clips with permission when you can get them.

**Length:** 30-60s. The number is the hook; the proof is the payoff.

**6-8 video ideas:**

1. "This [City] roofer went from 2 to 11 leads a month after we rebuilt his site" — hard number up front, then the screen proof.
2. Screen-recorded proof: client text "phone won't stop ringing" + Google call-clicks before vs after.
3. "Watch me build a $199/mo site for a local electrician" — process clip with a result attached (ties directly to the WaaS offer).
4. Client reaction / launch-day reveal, with permission — real face, real "wow."
5. "90 days after launch: here's this cleaning company's booking calendar now" (the before was empty).
6. "Same landscaper, same market — 3x the inbound calls after the rebuild. Here's the one change that did it."
7. "This HVAC owner almost didn't do it. Here's what his month looks like now." (handles the skeptical-of-agencies objection with proof.)
8. "How a $0-down site paid for itself in one job for this plumber" — the one-customer ROI math, made real.

**Keyword CTA:** "Want numbers like this for your shop? Comment **AUDIT** — I'll show you 3 things costing you calls right now, no pitch." Reuse these clips in DM follow-up and on the discovery call.

## Pillar 5 — Offer, Process & Founder BTS

**Funnel stage:** Capture / Close. **De-risk + conversion.** Kept to **~20% of posts. Soft-sell only — sell the free audit, never the build.**

**Purpose:** Show the real face and the real process that lets a skeptical stranger send a deposit. This is the trust bridge that de-risks the purchase and carries the free-audit CTA. Overt selling gets punished by the algorithm and by viewers — so even here we lead with value and de-risking, and the "ask" is only ever the free teardown.

**Best format:** **Talking head — your real, unpolished face.** Never hide your face on this pillar; hiding it caps you at low-ticket work, and high-ticket buyers book a *person*. Shoot it raw. Agency-gloss reads as an ad and kills reach.

**6-8 video ideas:**

1. "Free website teardown — send your link, I'll show you 3 things costing you customers. No pitch." (the core DM-magnet video.)
2. "How our 7-day website build actually works — $0 down, $199/mo, cancel anytime, you own it." (de-risk the WaaS offer, plainly.)
3. "The guarantee stack: don't pay until you love it, 90-day leads guarantee, cancel anytime — here's why I can offer that."
4. Day-in-the-life running a [City] web agency / a real client kickoff call.
5. "Taking 3 [City] home-service businesses this month — comment your city if you want a spot." (scarcity + local signal.)
6. "Why I build the website near-breakeven — and what I actually do after" (honest founder POV; frames the retainer without pitching it hard).
7. "What happens on a free teardown call — I'm just going to show you your 3 biggest leaks." (pre-frames the discovery call, kills the sales-y fear.)
8. "I only work with [City] trades — here's why that's better for you." (niche + de-risk in one.)

**Keyword CTA:** "Comment **AUDIT** (or DM me the word AUDIT) and I'll send your free teardown." See the funnel note below — the DM automation is what captures the lead.

### Capture mechanics that apply to every pillar's CTA

Native comment-to-DM automation is **not live in the US/UK/EU**, so don't tell people "I'll DM you" and rely on it firing automatically. Instead:

- Tell viewers to **"DM me the word AUDIT"** so ManyChat fires (Business account, DMs open to Everyone), or manually DM the commenters.
- **No clickable links are allowed in DMs.** ManyChat captures the owner's **email in-DM** and delivers the teardown + booking link **by email**.
- One keyword per video (AUDIT / ROAST / WEBSITE / GUIDE). Reply to hot comments publicly too so other owners see the offer.
- Bio = one keyword-rich line + **one** booking link that goes **straight to the UTM'd Calendly page** (with a "how did you find me?" field), not a homepage, not a Linktree.

## Signature Repeatable Formats

These are the named, batch-able formats you rerun forever. Each is a template — write the hook first, then fill the beats. **Batch-record 5-8 in one session** to sustain 4/week without filming daily.

### 1. Website Teardown
- **Pillar / stage:** 1 / Discover. **Format:** faceless screen-record + voiceover (hybrid for flagship). **Length:** 30-60s.
- **Beats:** (1) Hook naming the stakes in 2s → (2) show the real anonymized site → (3) call out **exactly 3 flaws** with your cursor (no CTA above the fold / phone not tap-to-call on mobile / hero headline that says nothing / 5-second load) → (4) mock or rebuild one fix → (5) keyword CTA.
- **On-screen text:** hook line top-third; number each flaw ("#1", "#2", "#3"); burn the keyword ("[City] [trade] website").
- **Hook bank:** "This is the worst [trade] website I've seen this week." / "This [City] plumber is losing calls because of this one button." / "POV: you paid your nephew $200 for this."
- **CTA:** "Comment ROAST for yours." **Frequency:** 6 / cycle.

### 2. Small Biz Site Roast ("Rating [niche] websites until I find one that doesn't need me")
- **Pillar / stage:** 1 / Discover. **Format:** faceless screen-record + voiceover. **Length:** 40-60s.
- **Beats:** Rapid-fire rate 3-5 anonymized [niche] sites out of 10, one flaw each, escalating — the open loop is "will any of them pass?" End on one that's genuinely decent (validates the niche, avoids pure negativity).
- **On-screen text:** running score ("6/10", "4/10"); niche + city keyword.
- **CTA:** "Comment your trade — I'll rate yours next." **Frequency:** ~2 / cycle.

### 3. UGC Roast ("Drop your website in the comments and I'll roast it")
- **Pillar / stage:** 1 / Discover→Capture. **Format:** talking-head hook → screen-record roasts as Video Replies. **Length:** 15-30s the ask; 30-60s each reply.
- **Why it wins:** infinite content, massive comment volume (ranking signal), and **every dropped link is a self-identified lead you can DM.** Each roast reply is its own searchable post.
- **Beats:** short talking-head ask → collect links in comments → reply to the best ones with a screen-record teardown → soft "want the full fix? DM AUDIT."
- **Frequency:** run once per cycle; the replies stretch across the week.

### 4. Before & After Redesign
- **Pillar / stage:** 2 / Engage. **Format:** split-screen or time-lapse, face-cam bubble optional. **Length:** 20-45s.
- **Beats:** (1) hook on the "before" ("this is a real [City] [trade] site") → (2) hold on the pain for 2s → (3) reveal the "after" (satisfying cut/transition) → (4) overlay a one-line result if you have it → (5) soft CTA.
- **On-screen text:** "BEFORE" / "AFTER" labels; result overlay ("5x the calls"); keyword.
- **CTA:** "Comment AUDIT for your before/after." **Frequency:** ~4 / cycle.

### 5. "$X vs $XXXX Website" ($500 vs $5,000)
- **Pillar / stage:** 2 / Engage + budget-qualify. **Format:** split-screen, hybrid bubble. **Length:** 30-60s.
- **Why it wins:** rides the no-gatekeeping/transparency trend, sparks comment debate, and **pre-qualifies budget** so the DMs you get already accept your range. Ties to the offer ladder (Starter vs Growth).
- **Beats:** show the cheap site's costs (looks fine, doesn't convert/get found) → show the real one (booking, click-to-call, local SEO, reviews) → land on "which one a serious [trade] actually needs."
- **On-screen text:** price tags; feature checklist ticking on the right side.
- **CTA:** "Comment AUDIT and I'll tell you which one your site is right now." **Frequency:** ~1 / cycle.

### 6. "Watch Me Build a Site in 60s"
- **Pillar / stage:** 2 / Engage (or 4 when tied to a client). **Format:** time-lapse screen-record + voiceover, face-cam bubble. **Length:** 45-60s.
- **Beats:** empty/Facebook-only start → speed-build the essentials on screen (hero, click-to-call, services, reviews, form) → live reveal → "this is a $199/mo build."
- **On-screen text:** stopwatch; label each block as it lands ("click-to-call", "booking").
- **CTA:** "Comment WEBSITE for how the 7-day, $0-down build works." **Frequency:** ~1-2 / cycle.

### 7. The Google-at-9pm Test
- **Pillar / stage:** 3 / Discover→Trust. **Format:** screen-record the search + talking-head framing. **Length:** 30-45s.
- **Beats:** "A customer just heard about you and typed your name into Google at 9pm" → screen-record the sad/empty/broken result → name the exact leak → the fix.
- **On-screen text:** "9:04 PM" clock; keyword "[trade] near me".
- **CTA:** "Comment your business name and I'll do this test for you." **Frequency:** recurring in Pillar 3 rotation.

### 8. Client Win / Case Study ("2 to 11 leads")
- **Pillar / stage:** 4 / Close. **Format:** talking-head number-first + screen-record proof. **Length:** 30-60s.
- **Beats:** hard number in the first 3s → what was broken before → the one change that mattered → the proof shot (call-clicks, client text) → soft CTA.
- **On-screen text:** the number, huge; "BEFORE / AFTER" on the metric.
- **CTA:** "Comment AUDIT for numbers like this." **Frequency:** ~2 / cycle. **Pin your best one.**

### 9. Free Teardown Offer
- **Pillar / stage:** 5 / Capture. **Format:** raw talking-head. **Length:** 15-30s.
- **Beats:** "Send me your link, I'll show you 3 things costing you customers — free, no pitch." → name what they'll get → the keyword ask.
- **On-screen text:** "FREE website teardown"; the keyword "AUDIT".
- **CTA:** "DM me the word AUDIT." **Frequency:** ~1 / cycle (the rest of Pillar 5 is BTS/process).

## Production Basics for a Solo Creator

The rule from the Spine: **shoot on a phone with good light and accurate burned-in captions.** Raw and opinionated beats glossy and agency-branded — over-production reads as an ad, lowers trust, and slows your cadence. Here's the minimum bar for each.

### Gear (keep it cheap and phone-first)

| Need | Do this | Don't bother with |
|------|---------|-------------------|
| Camera | Your phone's rear camera, 1080p/4K, vertical 9:16 | A DSLR / cinema rig |
| Stability | Any $20 tripod + phone clamp | Gimbals for talking-head |
| Light | Face a **window**, or one softbox/ring light in front (never behind) | Multi-point studio kits |
| Audio | A wired or wireless **lav mic** clipped ~8in below your chin | The phone's built-in mic for talking-head |
| Screen capture | **Screen Studio / Loom / CapCut** for teardowns + hybrid bubble | Pro NLEs (Premiere/DaVinci) |
| Editing + captions | **CapCut** (auto-captions, then correct them) | Anything with a learning curve |

### Lighting
- **Light hits your face, never behind you.** Backlight = a silhouette that reads as low-effort. Face a window during the day; add one soft light in front for night.
- Soft and even beats bright and harsh. A ring light or a lamp bounced off a wall is enough.
- Consistent background and framing across a series makes your feed look intentional without looking corporate.

### Audio (the one thing worth spending on)
- **Bad audio kills completion rate faster than bad video.** A cheap lav mic is the highest-ROI purchase you'll make.
- Record in a soft room (carpet, curtains, not a tiled bathroom). Kill the echo.
- For screen-record voiceovers, record the audio in the quiet room *after* you've captured the screen so your delivery is tight.

### Captions (non-negotiable — they feed SEO)
- **Burn captions into every video.** They add ~12% retention and TikTok indexes on-screen text (~30% of SEO weight).
- Auto-generate in CapCut, then **correct every word** — auto-captions mangle "[City]", trade names, and prices, and those are your exact keywords.
- Keep them mid-screen, high-contrast, 2-4 words at a time, sans-serif, big.

### On-screen text
- **Hook text in the first frame**, upper third (not hidden behind the TikTok UI on the right or bottom).
- **Burn your target keyword** on screen — say it out loud *and* show it (caption ~40%, on-screen ~30%, hashtags ~20%, audio ~10% of SEO weight). Say the **city and the niche in the first line.**
- Teardowns: use **cursor callouts, circles, and arrows** to point at each flaw. Number them ("#1, #2, #3").
- Before/afters: clear **BEFORE / AFTER** labels and a one-line **result overlay** on the "after."

### The hybrid setup (your money format)
For teardowns and "watch me build" videos, use the **face-cam bubble over a screen recording** (Screen Studio's built-in camera, or a phone-shot talking head composited in CapCut). Screen = proof of skill; face in the corner = the trust that sells a $3k-$6k build. Keep the bubble small, bottom corner, out from under the captions.

### Framing & delivery
- Vertical **9:16**, eyes in the **upper third**, a little headroom, framed chest-up.
- Hook must land in the **first 3 seconds** — open on the problem or the site, never "Hey guys, so today…".
- Keep it **30-90s**: 30-60s for tips/teardowns, up to 90s for a case study. Cut fast, no dead air, aim ~70% completion.
- **Original audio (you talking).** Only layer a trending sound on lighter BTS posts, and never let it drown out your spoken keywords.

### Batch workflow (how a solo creator actually sustains 4/week)
1. **Script day:** write 8-10 hooks first (hook decides everything). Pull scripts straight from your comments and DMs.
2. **Capture day:** batch-film all talking-heads in one lighting setup + outfit; screen-record all teardowns in one sitting.
3. **Edit day:** cut, auto-caption + correct, burn hook + keyword text, export.
4. **Schedule:** load the fixed Tue/Wed/Thu/Sat slots. **Never delete "low-view" videos** — they keep ranking in search and compounding for 6-12 months.
5. **Golden hour:** be live in the comments for the first 30-60 minutes of every post. Turn the best buyer question into a Video Reply — that's a free extra post *and* a searchable asset.

### Consistency checklist for every single video

- [ ] Hook lands in the first 3 seconds (problem or site on screen, no intro)
- [ ] City + niche said out loud in the first line
- [ ] Target keyword said aloud, in the caption (front-loaded), and burned on screen
- [ ] Captions burned in and corrected
- [ ] Leads with the outcome (calls/jobs/looking legit), not the feature
- [ ] Validates what the owner does right before showing the gap
- [ ] No named/identifiable business roasted (blurred or your own old work)
- [ ] No jargon, no hard-sell of the build — CTA sells the **free audit** only
- [ ] One keyword CTA (AUDIT / ROAST / WEBSITE / GUIDE) wired to the DM → email flow
- [ ] 3-5 hashtags: 1 broad + 2-3 niche + 1 local (#[City]smallbusiness)
- [ ] 30-90s, original audio, phone-shot and raw — not agency-glossy
