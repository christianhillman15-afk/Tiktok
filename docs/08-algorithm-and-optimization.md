# Algorithm & Growth Optimization

> **The core reframe:** You are **not** trying to go viral. You're trying to **rank for the exact questions a `[City]` small-business owner types into TikTok**, and convert a small, right-fit audience into booked calls. A 900-view video that books 3 calls beats a 90,000-view video that books zero. Optimize for **leads**, not vanity metrics.

TikTok in 2025–2026 runs as a **dual engine**:
- **For You (FYP)** = discovery. TikTok tests each new video with a small cold-start batch, then pushes it wider *only if the signals are good.* Spiky, algorithm-dependent.
- **Search** = intent, and this is where lead-gen compounds. A growing share of business owners use TikTok like Google. Search-optimized videos pull **300–500% more views** and keep ranking for **6–12 months** — 40–60% of an evergreen video's lifetime views come from search.

Your whole strategy: **treat every video as an evergreen search asset that answers one question an owner would type**, and engineer it to hold attention and capture a local lead.

---

## What actually drives distribution (in priority order)

1. **Completion rate / watch time — the #1 signal (~40–50% of the weight).** The effective bar for a second distribution push has crept up to **~70% completion** (it was ~50% in 2024). This is *the* reason short, tight videos beat long rambling ones for a niche account. **A 45-second video held at 75% gets pushed; a 3-minute ramble held at 30% gets buried.**
2. **Shares > Saves > Comments > Likes.** Saves are weighted heavily now because they feed Search and Collections — and for an agency, a **save = "I'll hire this person later,"** worth more than 1,000 likes. Shares expand reach fastest.
3. **Comments, especially in the first 30–60 minutes (the "golden hour").** A burst of early comments meaningfully increases reach; a high comment-to-view ratio can make a video **~4x** more likely to reach new audiences.
4. **Relevance beats follower count.** TikTok tests each video individually, so a **brand-new 200-follower local account can rank and book clients.** You do **not** need a following or a viral moment.

## TikTok SEO — where the buyers actually are

TikTok auto-transcribes your speech and indexes on-screen text and captions. Ranking weight (industry consensus):

| Signal | Approx. weight | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Caption keywords** | ~40% | Front-load the exact search phrase in the first line of the caption |
| **On-screen text** | ~30% | Burn the phrase into the opening frame |
| **Hashtags** | ~20% | 3–5 relevant tags (see below) |
| **Spoken audio / voiceover** | ~10% | **Say the phrase out loud in your first line** |

**The rule: say it, show it, caption it.** If you never say "website" or "`[City]`," you won't rank in Search — where the buyers are.

### Build a search-first content list
Brainstorm **30–50 exact phrases a `[City]` home-service owner would type**, then make **one video per phrase.** Examples:
- "how much does a small business website cost"
- "website vs Facebook page for my business"
- "why is my Google Business Profile not showing up"
- "why isn't my business on Google Maps"
- "best website for a plumber / HVAC / roofer"
- "do I need a website if I get business by word of mouth"

Mine your **comments and DMs** for the next 10 — every question a prospect asks is a video that ranks for the next person asking it.

### Keyword-optimized titles/captions (front-load the phrase)
- *"How much a small business website should cost in 2026"*
- *"Small business website vs just a Facebook page"*
- *"Why your business isn't showing up on Google Maps"*
- *"Best website setup for a `[City]` home-service business"*

## Length, cadence, and consistency

- **Ideal length for this niche: 30–90 seconds.** Long enough to teach one useful thing and earn a save; short enough to hold ~70% completion. Tips: 30–60s. Mini case studies: up to 90s. (TikTok's "viral sweet spot" hint is 21–34s, and 60–90s aligns with Creator Rewards eligibility.)
- **The hook must land in the first 3 seconds.** Open with the problem or the answer — never *"Hey guys, so today I wanted to talk about…"* See `05-hooks-library.md`.
- **Cadence: 4 posts/week on a fixed schedule** (e.g., Tue/Wed/Thu/Sat), sustained for **90 days before judging results.** Consistency trains the algorithm and grows your cold-start test batches. A steady 4/week beats 14 one week and 0 the next.
- **SEO ranking timeline:** appears in **1–3 days**, peaks at **7–14 days**, then compounds over 6–12 months. **Never delete "low-view" videos** — they're quietly ranking in Search.

See `03-content-calendar-30-days.md` for the batch-film-in-a-weekend plan.

## Hashtags & sounds in 2026

**Hashtags are a minor relevance/topic signal, not a reach lever.** The 20–30 tag spam approach is dead. Use **3–5**, weighted local + niche:
- 1 broad category anchor: `#smallbusinessmarketing`
- 2–3 niche: `#websitedesign` `#localseo` `#b2bmarketing`
- 1 local: `#[City]smallbusiness` or `#[City]`

Example set: `#websitedesign #localseo #smallbusinessmarketing #[City]smallbusiness #b2bmarketing`

**Sounds: default to original audio (you talking).** For B2B/authority content, your spoken keywords are what Search indexes — a trending sound drowns them out, dates the video, and reads as "corporate trying too hard." Use trending sounds **selectively** on lighter BTS/relatable posts only, and never let them bury your voice. Participation, not imitation.

## Account & conversion mechanics for local lead-gen

- **Business account** unlocks the clickable bio link (no 1,000-follower minimum), a contact/booking button, analytics, and ManyChat automation — but locks you to the royalty-free Commercial Music Library and tends to see lower reach (~2.5–4% vs ~11% for Creator accounts riding viral sounds). **Decide by your bottleneck:** if reach is the constraint, stay Creator; if conversion is the constraint (you have views but no system to capture them), go Business. (Details and the DM funnel live in `06-lead-funnel-and-dm-templates.md`.)
- **Geo-target locally.** TikTok supports **Zip Code and DMA targeting** for posts/ads — useful when you serve one metro. Going viral nationally is useless if you serve one city.
- **Instant Forms convert ~20% higher** than sending local-service traffic off to a website.
- **Comments are a second content surface.** Answer buyer-intent comments with a **Video Reply** — it becomes its own searchable post and surfaces the next lead.

## Pre-post optimization checklist (run on EVERY video)

- [ ] **Hook in first 3 seconds** — problem/answer, no intro
- [ ] **Target search phrase** said out loud in line 1, on-screen in frame 1, and front-loaded in the caption
- [ ] **Accurate burned-in captions** (adds ~12% retention *and* feeds SEO)
- [ ] **30–90s**, tight edit, aiming for ~70% completion
- [ ] **Value delivered** — one concrete, useful point (a save-worthy takeaway)
- [ ] **Two CTAs stacked:** engagement CTA first (*"comment your industry and I'll tell you your #1 site fix"*), then the fulfillment step (*"DM me the word AUDIT"*)
- [ ] **3–5 hashtags** (broad + niche + local)
- [ ] **Original audio** (or trending sound only if it doesn't bury your keywords)
- [ ] **`[City]` / niche stated out loud** so it ranks locally
- [ ] **Cover/thumbnail text** = the search phrase (helps Search browse + your profile grid)

## Working the "golden hour" (first 30–60 minutes)

1. Post at a consistent time your audience is active.
2. **Reply to every comment fast** — early comment velocity expands reach.
3. Ask a question in your caption or pinned comment to farm replies (*"comment your industry 👇"*).
4. Pin a comment with the next step (free audit / "DM me your city").
5. Turn the best buyer-question comment into a **Video Reply** = your next post.

## What to do when a video flops vs. pops

**When it pops (high views/completion):**
- **Make 3–5 follow-ups on the same topic immediately** — you found a search phrase people want.
- Pin it to your profile if it's proof or a strong teardown.
- Reply to buyer-intent comments with Video Replies to ride the wave.
- Check *why* it worked (hook? topic? format?) and clone the pattern.

**When it flops (low views/completion):**
- **Don't delete it** — it may still rank in Search over the next 1–3 weeks.
- Check the **completion-rate** stat first. Low completion = weak hook or too long. Re-cut a tighter version with a sharper first 3 seconds and re-post.
- If views are fine but no leads: the *content* landed but the *CTA* didn't — tighten the offer/CTA.
- One flop means nothing. Judge on the **90-day trend**, not single videos.

## What to measure (and what to ignore)

**Measure:** saves, shares, profile visits, link clicks, comment *questions*, DMs, and **discovery calls booked.**
**Ignore as primary metrics:** raw view count and follower count.

Full metric list, benchmarks, and the tracking sheet are in `09-tracking-and-tools.md`. The North-Star reminder: **the right 20 buyers, not 200,000 random viewers.**

## Pitfalls

- **Chasing views/followers instead of leads** — broad "marketing tips" content attracts other marketers, not buyers.
- **Videos too long/slow** so completion tanks.
- **Weak or missing hook** — buries the point behind an intro and fails the 3-second test.
- **Relying on hashtags alone** — not saying/showing your keywords means you never rank in Search.
- **Over-produced, corporate-glossy content** — reads as an ad, underperforms raw founder-to-camera, and slows your cadence.
- **Hashtag spam** (20–30 tags) and irrelevant trending tags.
- **Forcing trending sounds** onto authority content.
- **No local signal or CTA** — never stating your city means views never become booked calls.
- **Inconsistency / deleting flops** — stopping at 3 weeks or removing videos that are quietly ranking.
- **Ignoring comments/DMs** — that's where buyers raise their hand.
