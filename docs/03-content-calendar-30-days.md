# 30-Day Content Calendar

Your first 30 days of posting, mapped day by day. This is the launch sprint that sits at the front of the 90-day window — so it runs **near-daily (1 post/day)** on purpose. Near-daily posting builds a bigger cold-start test batch, stacks a searchable video library fast, and trains the algorithm on your niche quicker than a slow start. **From Day 31 you drop to the sustained 4-posts-per-week fixed schedule (Tue/Wed/Thu/Sat)** that the strategy spine calls for and hold it for the rest of the 90 days. Don't judge results before Day 30 — TikTok SEO ranking peaks 7–14 days *per video* and compounds for 6–12 months.

Everything here stays inside the canonical strategy: the websites-first wedge for home-service & trades owners **anywhere in the US**, the five content pillars, the free-audit lead magnet as the one thing the whole engine points at, and the raw founder-to-camera voice. Our market is national — we niche by **trade**, not by city — but every client is still local, so we keep the "your town / near me / show up on Google when a customer searches at 9pm" framing whenever it's about *their* market. We sell the free teardown in-video — **never the build**.

## How to read this

- **Swap the sample trade** (plumber, HVAC, roofer, electrician, landscaper, cleaner) for whichever vertical you're filming. The calendar rotates trades on purpose so no two weeks look the same and every trade sees a video that's clearly for *them*. Keep each video's lane narrow — the point is that a plumber thinks "that's my site" and everyone else scrolls. Niche by trade, not by metro: we serve these owners across the US.
- **Say the trade and the specific problem out loud in the first line, put it in the caption (front-loaded), and burn it into on-screen text.** That's what ranks you in Search, where the buyers are — and "plumber website" pulls buying-intent owners from every metro in the country, not just one.
- **Every post gets a pinned comment** with the next step (free audit / "DM me AUDIT") and **3–5 hashtags**: `#websitedesign #homeservicebusiness #tradesbusiness #localseo #smallbusinessmarketing`. Keep the tags trade/niche, not city — the reach is national.
- **Work your own comments hard in the first 30–60 minutes** (golden hour). Answer buyer-intent questions with a **Video Reply** — that becomes its own searchable post (and your optional 2nd post of the day).

### Pillar key

| Tag | Pillar | Funnel role |
|---|---|---|
| **Teardown** | Website Teardowns / Roasts | Top — REACH + authority (highest volume) |
| **Before/After** | Before / After Redesign Reveals | Mid — DESIRE + buying intent |
| **Education** | Objection-Busting & Plain-Language Education | Search-first — TRUST + ranking |
| **Proof** | Proof / Client Results & Case Studies | Bottom — PROOF that closes |
| **Offer/BTS** | Offer, Process & Founder BTS | CONVERSION (kept to ~20% of posts) |

### Format key

- **Hybrid** = face-cam bubble over a screen recording (Loom/Screen Studio/CapCut) — the money format; screen proves skill, face builds trust.
- **Faceless SR+VO** = screen recording + voiceover only — for high-volume teardowns/ratings, lowest friction.
- **Time-lapse** = sped-up redesign with a satisfying reveal at the end (drop your face in at the reveal).
- **Talking-head** = phone, you to camera — for opinions, myth-busting, offer, and BTS.

### CTA key

Rotates soft → direct. **Note:** native comment-to-DM automation isn't live in US/UK/EU, so the ask is always "**DM me the word AUDIT**" (fires ManyChat, which captures email and sends the teardown + booking link *by email* — no links in DMs). Comment CTAs still fuel the golden hour.

## How to batch-film this in a weekend

You cannot film 30 videos one at a time and keep your cadence — you'll quit by Day 6. Batch by **format**, not by day, so you're not resetting your setup and headspace 30 times. One focused weekend gets you **~2 weeks of content (12–15 videos)**; run two weekend sessions to cover the month, or push through all 30 in one hard weekend if you're going all in.

**Gear (keep it raw):** phone on a tripod, one soft light or a window at your face, lav or clean room audio, and a screen-recording tool (Screen Studio / Loom / CapCut) for the face-cam-bubble hybrid. Burn in accurate captions on every export (they add ~12% retention and feed Search). Original audio — you talking — beats trending sounds for authority content.

### Saturday — capture day

**Block A (morning, ~2 hrs) — Screen recordings, no face.** Line up 8–10 real home-service & trades sites from anywhere in the US (blur the brand) so you're rotating trades, not repeating one. Record each teardown: hook naming the stakes in 2 seconds → show the site → call out exactly 3 flaws with your cursor (no click-to-call, weak hero headline, slow mobile load) → the fix. This covers all your Teardown + rating days. Also screen-record your before/after redesigns and client-proof screens (call-clicks, the "phone won't stop ringing" text) in this same block.

**Block B (midday, ~2 hrs) — Talking-head, batch by outfit.** Sit down once and knock out every Education, Offer, and BTS script back to back: the cost video, the Facebook-page objection, the Google-at-9pm test, word-of-mouth-ends-on-Google, the "$500 vs $5k vs $50k," the free-teardown offer, the "3 spots this month" call, and the Day-30 recap. Change your shirt once halfway through so 30 days of posts don't look filmed in one sitting.

**Block C (afternoon, ~1 hr) — Reveals & reactions.** Record any face-to-camera reveal moments for the before/afters and the client launch-day reaction (with permission). These are your highest-save mid-funnel posts.

### Sunday — edit & schedule day

- Add face-cam bubbles to the teardowns/proof (hybrid), export time-lapses for the redesigns.
- Burn in captions, front-load the target keyword (the trade) in every caption, write the pinned-comment CTA and drop in the 3–5 hashtags.
- Schedule the whole batch to post at a fixed daily time so cadence never depends on your mood or your calendar.
- Save 2–3 filler slots empty on purpose — you'll fill them with **Video Replies** mined from real comments once you're live (see the review ritual).

**Rule:** never let editing perfectionism slow the cadence. A raw phone video at 75% completion beats a polished one that never ships. Consistency trains the algorithm more than production value.

## Weekly review ritual (every Sunday, 30 minutes)

Judge the week on **leads, not vanity.** Views and followers are not the scoreboard — qualified DMs and booked calls are.

**1. Score the week against the spine's KPIs:**
- Qualified DMs + keyword comments (AUDIT / WEBSITE intent) — the primary top-of-funnel signal.
- Discovery calls booked (attributed via the UTM'd Calendly link + "how did you find me?" field).
- **Saves + shares per video** — the highest-value signals; a save means "I'll hire this person later."
- Completion rate (target ~70%) and search impressions on your target phrases (e.g. "plumber website," "HVAC website").
- Wedge clients closed + how many are on track for the Growth Partner retainer by month 3.

**2. Find the pattern.** Which hook style, pillar, format, and *trade* pulled the most saves and DMs? Do more of it next week. Which fell flat? Adjust the hook — **do not delete it.** "Flops" quietly rank in Search and compound for months; deleting them throws away future search traffic.

**3. Mine comments & DMs for next week's scripts.** Every question a prospect typed ("what would this cost for a 2-truck HVAC company?") is a video that will rank for the next owner in that trade who asks it — anywhere in the country. Pull 5–10 new script ideas straight from this week's comments and slot the best ones as **Video Replies** into next week's empty slots.

**4. Clear the funnel.** Every AUDIT comment/DM answered same-day; every teardown request turned into a personalized 2–3 flaw audit (withhold the full fixes for the live call); every discovery-call recap sent within 24 hours. A hot teardown is worthless if the DMs go cold.

**5. Confirm cadence adherence.** Did you hit your posts this week? Cadence is a tracked KPI. If the batch is running low, block next weekend's film session **now** — the sprint only works if the schedule never breaks.

> After Day 30: keep the exact same pillar rotation and CTA ladder, just step down to the sustained **4 posts/week (Tue/Wed/Thu/Sat)** and run the weekly review for the rest of the 90-day window before you re-evaluate the whole strategy.
